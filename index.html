Acme Industries

Engineering Team

We build and run the software that keeps Acme's products shipping: the customer platform, the internal tools our factories depend on, and the pipelines that move data between them.

What we are responsible for

  • Ship product. Design, build, and release the Acme customer platform and its mobile clients.
  • Keep it running. On-call rotation, incident response, and the service level targets we publish each quarter.
  • Own the pipeline. Source control, automated builds, tests, and deployments for every service we run.
  • Guard the data. Access control, backups, retention, and the audit trail behind them.
  • Support the business. Internal tooling for Operations, Finance, and the plant floor.
